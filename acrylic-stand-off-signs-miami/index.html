How raised acrylic signs work

Acrylic stand off signs Miami businesses consider for offices and reception areas use space between the sign panel and the wall. That gap creates a dimensional effect, rather than having the panel sit flat against its background. The result can suit a logo, business name or other concise information where a simple, polished wall display is the goal.

The design needs to work with the room as well as the artwork. Panel size, color, letter or logo detail, wall surface and viewing distance all influence how the finished sign reads. Bring a photo of the wall and your logo file when you discuss the project, if you have them.

Key facts before production

A useful project brief covers the sign's purpose, approximate dimensions, wall location, artwork, colors and any building or property requirements. If the sign will carry a logo, provide a clean, high-resolution file when available.

  • What it covers: Artwork review, sign sizing, material and finish choices, and a mounting plan suited to the project.
  • Typical timing: Timing varies with design approval, material selection, production needs and site coordination. Ask for a project schedule once those details are clear.
  • What affects cost: Size, acrylic thickness, design complexity, finish, mounting hardware, quantity and installation needs can all affect the overall cost. Request a quote based on your actual specifications.

When comparing providers, ask who checks the artwork, what mounting details are included, and whether installation is part of the proposal or handled separately. Compare the same dimensions and finish options rather than choosing from a general description alone.

Planning for a Miami wall display

For a Miami office or reception area, start with the actual wall where people will see the sign. Wall material, access to the installation area and any building rules can affect the mounting plan. A few terms may come up during planning. Stand-offs are spacers that hold a panel away from the wall. Acrylic is the rigid plastic panel used for the sign face.
